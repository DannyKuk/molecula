Molecula can't tell you what to do with your own medicines: whether to start, stop or change one, which dose to take, or whether it is safe for you. That depends on your health and your other medicines, so your doctor or pharmacist decides it with you.

What Molecula can do is explain what a drug's label says in general: how it works, its usual dosing, its warnings and interactions, and what it says about pregnancy or surgery. Ask, for example: "What does the ibuprofen label say about pregnancy?"
