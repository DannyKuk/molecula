# A 3D request in a follow-up turn, 2026-10-18

On 2026-10-13, in a conversation that had just shown imatinib in 3D, "Show me acetaminophen in 3D" was answered without a tool call, saying Molecula had no 3D structure of acetaminophen. No view appeared, because the view only opens for a `get_complex` result (#173). Does a 3D request in a follow-up turn still skip `get_complex`?

## Setup

- **Chat:** `ChatAgent.stream` as `main.py` builds it, with `OFFLINE=1`, the built store and the bge-m3 index, without the guard (`python -m evals.chat_model.followup_3d`). The first question is answered by the agent in each run and goes back as text only, as the frontend sends earlier turns (`conversationWith` in `frontend/lib/chat-turn.ts`).
- **Model:** `openai/gpt-oss-20b` (MXFP4) in LM Studio, 32,768 tokens of context, one parallel slot, answers at medium reasoning effort. Main's prompt and tools at d336293.
- **Conversations:** `backend/evals/datasets/followup_3d.yaml`: five two-turn conversations, the follow-up asking for a drug with a reviewed complex (`found`) or without one (`no_complex`, the molecule alone), one in German, one after a label question. Each asked 5 times; each follow-up also asked 5 times as the first question of a new chat.
- **Scoring:** by script, whether the follow-up's `get_complex` result names the expected drug. A regex counts answers that mention the view and sentences that say there is no 3D structure; every answer was also read.

## Result

| Conversation | Follow-up called `get_complex` | Mentions the view | Says there is no 3D | As a first question: `get_complex` / view |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| imatinib → "Show me acetaminophen in 3D" (`no_complex`) | 5 of 5 | 5 | 0 | 5 of 5 / 5 |
| imatinib → "Show me ibuprofen in 3D" (`found`) | 5 of 5 | 4 | 0 | 5 of 5 / 5 |
| acetaminophen → "Show me imatinib in 3D" (`found`) | 5 of 5 | 5 | 0 | 5 of 5 / 5 |
| German imatinib → "Zeig mir Paracetamol in 3D" (`no_complex`) | 5 of 5 | 5 | 0 | 5 of 5 / 5 |
| "What is ibuprofen used for?" → "Show me ibuprofen in 3D" | 5 of 5 | 5 | 0 | as row 2 |

No errors. Median 11 to 14 s per follow-up (including the first turn), 5.5 to 9 s as a first question.

## What it shows

- **The bug no longer shows.** Every follow-up called `get_complex` for the right drug, and none said the drug has no 3D structure. Six sentences say there is no structure of the drug bound to a target, which is true for `no_complex`.
- **The likely fix is #232** (2026-10-16, three days after the report), which added the rule in `prompts/agent/system.md` that the chat shows the view of every `get_complex` result, the molecule alone for `no_complex`, and that an answer never says a 3D model can't be shown. The answers have also reasoned at medium since #254.
- **The one answer without a view mention** describes ibuprofen's contacts in 4PH9 without saying they are shown below.

## Limits

- 25 follow-ups and one model. The original miss was seen once; 0 of 25 doesn't rule out a rare one.
- The `no_complex` answers describe acetaminophen's ring and groups from memory and cite the result for it, once with "two phenyl rings" (it has one). That is a different problem, filed as #265 and fixed below.

## The molecule alone (#265), 2026-10-19

The 3D rule in `prompts/agent/system.md` said "Describe what the structure shows" for every `get_complex` result. A `no_complex` result holds only the drug's name and CID, so the model described the molecule from memory. The rule now says what to describe with `found` (the structure, from the result), and that with `no_complex` the answer says the view shows the molecule alone and that Molecula has no reviewed structure of the drug bound to its target, and stops there.

Same setup on main at db4530d (the prompt and tools changed since d336293, so the before was run again). A regex counts the `no_complex` answers that name parts of the molecule (rings, groups, atoms, bonds, hydroxyl, amide and their German words); every answer was also read, and the regex matched the ones that describe it.

| `no_complex` answers (acetaminophen, 4 rows × 5) | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Describe the molecule | 17 of 20 | 0 of 20 |
| Mention the view | 20 | 19 |
| Cite a number no result in the turn carries | 12 | 5 |

Before, one answer put the hydroxyl at the meta position (it is para) and another described the stick colours, which the view decides. After, each answer is one or two sentences: "The 3D view shows acetaminophen alone; Molecula has no reviewed structure of it bound to a target." The `found` rows are unchanged: 25 of 25 called `get_complex` for the right drug, and the answers describe the hydrogen bonds and contacts the result returns. The quick safety eval (`python -m evals.safety.run --quick`) passed 15 of 15.
