# Answers about a drug without a label, 2026-10-14

How does the chat answer about a drug that Molecula has no US label for (#182, #192)? Metamizole is the only one of the 134 stored drugs without a label: it isn't approved in the US (`data/scope.yaml`). Molecula has its PubChem properties and one ChEMBL target, cyclooxygenase, with the mechanism "Cyclooxygenase inhibitor". It has nothing on what metamizole is used for, its side effects or its warnings. #182 saw a comparison answer fill those in from memory, partly wrong. The baseline is `main` at 590ae1b; the two fixes tried for #193 follow it as a change log.

## Setup

- **Chat:** `POST /chat` in-process as `main.py` wires it, guard included, with `OFFLINE=1` and the built store and bge-m3 index (`python -m evals.chat_model.no_label --times 3`). A two-turn question is asked turn by turn, and the first answer is sent back as the frontend sends it. Only the last answer is scored.
- **Model:** `openai/gpt-oss-20b` (MXFP4) in LM Studio, llama.cpp ROCm runtime on an RX 7900 XTX, 32,768 tokens of context, one parallel slot, temperature 0.2.
- **Questions:** 11 in `backend/evals/datasets/no_label_questions.yaml`, each asked 3 times, so 33 answers. 8 are about metamizole under four of its names (metamizole, dipyrone, Novalgin, Novaminsulfon): its uses, its side effects, comparisons with imatinib, ibuprofen and paracetamol, and two follow-ups like the one in #182. 3 of the 8 are in German. 3 controls compare two drugs that both have labels.
- **Scoring:** each answer is judged by hand against two rules (`RULES` in `backend/evals/chat_model/no_label.py`). *From memory*: it states a drug's class, use, effect, side effect, warning, dosing or structure that no returned label passage or tool result holds. *Denies data*: it says Molecula doesn't cover or has no information on a supported drug, or that it can't answer what a tool returns, such as metamizole's mechanism from ChEMBL. An answer passes when it breaks neither rule and arrived. The verdicts, with a note on each answer, are in `backend/evals/chat_model/no_label_verdicts/`. The script also counts hints for the judge, such as words like "pain", "fever" or "NSAID" that appear in no returned passage.

## Result

Baseline, main 590ae1b, no errors:

| Questions | Answers | Pass | From memory | Denies data | Cite a label passage | Seconds p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Metamizole | 24 | **13** | 2 | **9** | 1 | 7.2 |
| Controls | 9 | 5 | 4 | 0 | 6 | 12.6 |

## What it shows

- **Denying the drug is the common failure, not filling it in.** 9 of 24 answers say Molecula has no data on metamizole: "Molecula's data does not include information on dipyrone", "in meiner Datenbank nicht unterstützt". That is wrong, because its properties and mechanism are stored. These answers also drop the other drug of a comparison, although its label was searched. All 3 askings of "How does dipyrone compare to ibuprofen?" and of the German Novaminsulfon/paracetamol question end this way. The prompt's rule "say that Molecula's data doesn't cover it" probably invites it.
- **Filling in from memory happens when only `compare_drugs` ran.** "Compare imatinib and metamizole." got a "Drug class: NSAID, analgesic/antipyretic" row and "used for pain and fever relief" in 2 of 3 askings. The tool returns properties only, and nothing told the model that metamizole has no label.
- **Single-drug questions are answered right.** "What is metamizole used for?" and "What are the side effects of Novalgin?" get "no US label, so I can't say", 6 of 6.
- **The controls fill in too.** 4 of 9 add something no passage holds: ibuprofen and naproxen dosing intervals, naproxen's uses "from the label" when no returned passage had them, metformin as "a biguanide".

## Limits

- One judge (the session that wrote the eval) and 3 askings per question. The counts show which failure is common, not a precise rate. For #193 the baseline was asked 3 more times with the same setup: askings 4 to 6 gave 14 of 24 passes, 4 from memory and 7 denials, against 13, 2 and 9 for askings 1 to 3, and the controls 4 of 9 against 5 of 9. A difference of two or three answers between two runs is within that spread.
- The rules don't count an answer that drops the other drug of a comparison. It is in the notes: on main every denial also drops the other drug.
- #182 was seen in Danny's app while LM Studio had gpt-oss-20b loaded with 8,192 tokens of context and 4 parallel slots. These runs use the documented 32,768 tokens and one slot, so the reported case may have had less context.

## Reproduce

```
cd backend
OFFLINE=1 LOCAL_LLM_BASE_URL=http://localhost:1234/v1 LOCAL_LLM_MODEL=openai/gpt-oss-20b \
    uv run python -m evals.chat_model.no_label --times 3
```

Answers are cached in `data/build/eval/no_label/`, keyed by the model, every prompt file and the tool descriptions. A re-run with the same setup asks nothing and prints the table from the verdicts. A new setup writes a review sheet next to the cache to judge from.

## Changes since this baseline

Each row is a change tried for #193, measured on the same 33 questions. None beat the baseline, so none is on `main`. Change 2 is on branch `fix/182-attempts` (49ebc59). Change 1 was not committed: its prompt is described in the table, and its verdicts can't be re-run from a checkout.

| Change | Metamizole: pass | From memory | Denies data | Controls pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Baseline, main 590ae1b | 13 of 24 | 2 | 9 | 5 of 9 |
| Baseline asked again (askings 4 to 6) | 14 of 24 | 4 | 7 | 4 of 9 |
| 1. Prompt: class, uses, side effects, warnings and dosing come only from label passages, also in a comparison; say which part isn't covered, never that a found drug isn't; a drug in `drugs_without_label` is covered, give the other tools' data and say there is no label. `search_labels` and `compare_drugs` docstrings say the same | 13 of 24 | 8 | 3 | 7 of 9 |
| 2. Change 1, plus `has_label` on `get_properties` and `compare_drugs` results, and the no-label rule names `get_properties` and `get_targets` and forbids describing the drug at all | 11 of 24 | 11 | 2 | 6 of 9 (1 error) |
| 3. Main's prompt; each `drugs_without_label` entry of a `search_labels` result carries the drug's ChEMBL targets and mechanism, and the docstring says what it holds and that Molecula has nothing on the drug's uses, side effects or warnings | 10 of 24 | 10 | 4 | 5 of 9 |

- **Both changes traded denials for memory.** Told that a drug without a label is still covered and that the answer should say the label is missing, the model talks about the drug, and adds what it knows: "used in other countries as a pain and fever reducer", "a sulfonamide" (it is a sulfonate). Once it listed made-up properties (CID 5281, 247.3 g/mol) without calling `get_properties`. Change 2's explicit "don't describe it at all, not even as an opening sentence" didn't stop it.
- **What did work:** the denials fell from 9 to 2 or 3, comparisons now answer the label drug with citations, and the controls improved. The fix needs to keep that without the memory. #193 lists the ideas not tried yet.
- Change 2 put a prompt change and a tool change in one run, so neither is known alone.
- **Change 3 gave the model data instead of instructions, with the same result.** With the mechanism in the search result, the answers give it ("ChEMBL records it as a COX inhibitor") and then add "a pain and fever reliever", "an NSAID", agranulocytosis. Memory rose from 2 and 4 in the two baseline runs to 10, well outside the spread. Three different changes, one shape: whenever gpt-oss-20b has something to say about metamizole, it adds what it knows.
- **Change 3 also showed a citation defect.** The model cites the ChEMBL mechanism with a label passage's number: `[1]` points at an ibuprofen or metformin passage, or at no passage when none was returned (5 answers). Anything the model may cite from a `search_labels` result needs its own reference, or must come from another tool. Change 3 is on branch `fix/193-no-label-answers` (2008be8).
- **A separate pattern:** every denial of "Was ist der Unterschied zwischen Novaminsulfon und Paracetamol?" in change 3 searched only Novaminsulfon, found no passage and stopped without searching paracetamol. That is how the search is formed for a comparison, not the memory/denial trade.
